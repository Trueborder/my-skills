# Before publishing

- Identify the audience and the decision or action the text supports. Use the product's terminology and keep status, limitations and warnings meaningful in each target language.
- Check product features, compatibility, prices, availability, performance, seller details, release dates and support promises against the sources that own those facts. Use the applicable product materials, current release information or official records. Treat an unverified claim as open.
- Preserve dates, qualifiers, links and disclosures. Make the task and intended reader concrete. Remove unsupported trust claims, testimonials and broad promises.
- In review-only mode give the claim, the evidence or missing source, the consequence and the suggested correction. Surface material factual errors separately from readability suggestions. Follow the author's punctuation and style where the wording remains clear.

Adapted from the factual-accuracy, voice and company-fit checks in the locally installed De-Slop workflow.
