# Context profile: support

## When this fires

Account access, registration, activation, recovery, billing, device approval, troubleshooting, and help pages for LongJumpReplay.

## Register

Direct and calm. State the next action, then explain any required detail or consequence. Use plain Czech or English matching the page. Keep password, recovery, payment, and licence guidance precise. Acknowledge when data is still loading, unavailable, stale, or needs confirmation.

## Format and channel

- Give one clear action at a time where a flow has multiple steps.
- Name the field, code, device, or account area the user needs.
- Explain technical or security terms in plain language when users need them to decide.
- Do not promise a purchase, licence, delivery, recovery, or approval succeeded unless the application has verified that result.
- Do not ask for passwords, one-time codes, or reusable activation secrets in support copy.
- Keep security and fallback instructions that protect the user's account.

## What done looks like

A user can identify the right account action, understand its effect, and know what to do if the displayed status is uncertain.
