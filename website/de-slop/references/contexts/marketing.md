# Context profile: marketing

## When this fires

Public product/studio pages, product catalog descriptions, and calls to explore or open products on tomaspisar.cz.

## Register

Calm and practical. Introduce what the product helps a person do, then describe the relevant workflow. No hype, urgency, or unsupported performance claims.

## Format and channel

- Put the product or user task in the heading.
- Use short paragraphs and concrete verbs.
- Name platforms and source data accurately when relevant.
- Keep calls to action literal, such as viewing product details or opening an app.
- Preserve Czech/English meaning and each page's language.

## What done looks like

A visitor can identify which product fits their task and where to continue. This profile does not replace product fact checking.

## Voice example

"Review long-jump take-offs with live video replay. Capture stays running while you freeze, replay, and decide."

Source: `tomaspisar.cz/index.html`.
