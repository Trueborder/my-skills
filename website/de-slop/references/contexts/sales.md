# Context profile: sales

## When this fires

LongJumpReplay product pricing, licence options, checkout guidance, activation and post-purchase pages on tomaspisar.cz.

## Register

Factual, restrained and buyer-focused. State what the product does, supported plans, platform, device limits, price and checkout conditions exactly as the current product pages provide them. Distinguish advertised availability from a verified purchase or created licence.

## Format and channel

- Put product and licence facts before broad benefit language.
- Keep supported claims specific to current product documentation.
- Describe checkout/security status only when the implementation verifies it.
- Avoid guarantees that a downloaded installer is safe based only on its name or source link; state a verifiable publisher or integrity check if available.
- Keep purchase terms, activation requirements, eligibility and refund guidance visible and consistent.
- Do not invent discounts, urgency, testimonials or outcomes.

## What done looks like

A buyer can compare the stated licence options and understand the next step without being promised an unverified payment or activation outcome.
